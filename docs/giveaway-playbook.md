Internal. Link: `https://www.csplumbinglee.com/water-heater-giveaway`.
Open now through 11:59 PM ET, December 8, 2026. Winner announced on or about
December 15. Technical notes: `docs/forms-and-photos.md`.

**The contest (Aiden, 2026-11-17):** we are looking for the oldest working
water heater in Cape Coral, Fort Myers and North Fort Myers. The oldest one,
confirmed on site, gets replaced with a new one, installed free. People can
enter their own or one they know of (with the owner's OK); the prize goes to
the homeowner.

## Why we are doing it

The prize is one install. The return is the list: every entry is a
homeowner's address, phone, email, heater age and a photo. A heater 10+
years old is a replacement lead (the office email tags it).

## Judging

1. List every entry (sheet, or the `Giveaway Entry:` emails) and sort by
   year, oldest first. For "not sure" entries, read the label photo: the
   serial number carries the date.
2. Remove duplicates per home and anything outside Cape Coral, Fort Myers
   and North Fort Myers.
3. Visit the three to five oldest. Photograph the data plate, confirm the
   date and confirm the heater is connected and making hot water. An entry
   whose date cannot be confirmed cannot win.
4. Oldest confirmed date wins. A tie is broken by a recorded random drawing
   among the tied entries.
5. Before announcing: confirm ownership on the county property appraiser
   site, and that the homeowner accepts the prize (matters most for entries
   submitted by someone else).
6. Homeowner signs a no-charge work order (included scope, any extras
   quoted), a photo release and a W-9 (prize value is over the 1099
   threshold).
7. Announce with first name, city and the old heater's year only. Keep the
   entry list and the site-visit photos.
