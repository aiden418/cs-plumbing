# Water heater giveaway: launch playbook

Internal. Link: `https://www.csplumbinglee.com/water-heater-giveaway`.
Open now through 11:59 PM ET, December 8, 2026. Drawing on or about
December 10. Technical notes: `docs/forms-and-photos.md`.

## Why we are doing it

The prize is one install. The return is the list: every entry is a
homeowner's address, phone, email, heater age and a photo. A heater 10+
years old is a replacement lead (the office email tags it).

## The drawing

1. Export or list every entry (sheet, or the `Giveaway Entry:` emails).
   Remove duplicates per household and anything outside Lee, Charlotte or
   Collier.
2. Number the rows. Screen-record a random number generator picking one.
   Keep the recording and the list.
3. Before announcing: confirm ownership on the county property appraiser
   site, then a site visit to confirm the heater matches the photo.
4. Winner signs a no-charge work order (included scope, any extras quoted),
   a photo release and a W-9 (prize value is over the 1099 threshold).
5. Announce with first name and city only.
