# Terms & Conditions on quotes, proposals and invoices (internal)

The public Terms & Conditions live at **https://www.csplumbinglee.com/terms**. Every
estimate, bid proposal, work order and invoice cites them so the customer can read the
governing terms before accepting. This note is the office checklist for doing that.

Source of truth in code: `src/lib/terms-meta.ts` (revision, URL, the sentence below) and
`src/lib/terms.ts` (clause text). `docs/` is the one intentional copy of the sentence;
update it when the revision changes.

## 1. The sentence to print on every document

Put it on page 1, next to the license numbers, in the same type size as the body.

**Estimate / quote**

> This estimate is subject to C&S Plumbing of Lee's Terms & Conditions rev. 2026-11-02,
> available at https://www.csplumbinglee.com/terms and incorporated by reference. Signing,
> paying, scheduling, or authorizing the work constitutes acceptance.

**Bid proposal** — same sentence with "This proposal is subject to …".

**Invoice** — same sentence with "This invoice is subject to …", plus the credit-card line:

> Credit card payments carry a 3% surcharge (credit cards only; no surcharge on cash, check,
> ACH or debit). Balances unpaid after the due date accrue 1.5% per month.

Generated by `termsSubjectLine("estimate" | "proposal" | "invoice")` in `src/lib/terms-meta.ts`.

## 2. Citation format

`Terms rev. YYYY-MM-DD` — the value of `TERMS_VERSION`. The revision on the document is the
one that governs that job, even after a newer revision is published, so never leave it off
and never back-fill an old document with a newer revision.

## 3. Publishing a new revision

1. Edit clause text in `src/lib/terms.ts`.
2. Bump `TERMS_VERSION` and `TERMS_EFFECTIVE_DATE` in `src/lib/terms-meta.ts` (same date).
3. Add a `TERMS_REVISIONS` entry in `src/lib/terms.ts` saying what changed.
4. Deploy. Open `/terms`, click **Print / Save as PDF**, file the PDF in the office Drive as
   `CS-Terms-rev-YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`. Prior revisions are kept there and in git; the site shows
   only the current one and the office sends a prior revision on request.
5. Update the revision string in the invoicing software template, the proposal renderer
   (`cs-proposal-pdf` skill footer and acceptance clause) and `csplumbing-assets`
   `references/payment-terms.md`.
6. Update section 1 of this file.

## 4. Notices that must be printed on the signed document

A link to `/terms` does **not** satisfy these. Each is reproduced on the Terms page
(section 20) for the customer's information, but the statute wants it in the contract.
Have the attorney confirm the verbatim text and applicability before relying on them.

| Statute | When | Where on the document | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| FS 713.015 Construction Lien Law notice | Direct contract with an owner over $2,500 to improve a residence of up to four units | First page, or a separate page signed and dated by the owner | At least 12-point, capitalized, bold |
| FS 489.1425 Homeowners' Construction Recovery Fund notice | Any residential repair, restoration, improvement or construction contract over $2,500 (covers Division II / plumbing since July 1, 2016) | In the contract, followed by the Construction Industry Licensing Board address and phone | Statutory wording "substantially in the following form" |
| FS 558.005 Chapter 558 notice | Contracts for construction or remodeling of a dwelling | In the contract | Capital letters |
| FS 501.031 Home solicitation cancellation notice | Sale solicited and signed at the customer's home, over $25, not customer-initiated repair/maintenance and not a written emergency waiver | Immediately next to the buyer's signature | At least 10-point bold |
| FS 489.119(5)(b) License number | Every proposal, contract, invoice and advertisement | Header or footer | CFC1432485 and CFC057076 |
| Fixed Price clause (brand rule) | Any document quoting a price for a defined scope | Contract terms block | Wording in `src/lib/terms.ts`, clause 3.3 |

Fines for a missing Recovery Fund notice: up to $500 for a first violation, $1,000 after.
