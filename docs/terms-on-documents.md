# Terms & Conditions on quotes, proposals and invoices (internal)

The public Terms & Conditions live at **https://www.csplumbinglee.com/terms**. Every
estimate, bid proposal, work order and invoice cites them so the customer can read the
governing terms before accepting. This note is the office checklist for doing that.

Source of truth in code: `src/lib/terms-meta.ts` (revision, URL, the sentence below) and
`src/lib/terms.ts` (clause text). This file is the one intentional copy of the sentence;
update it when the revision changes.

## 1. The sentence to print on every document

Put it on page 1, next to the license numbers, in the same type size as the body, and make
sure the customer can open the link before they accept. Keep the accepted copy (signed PDF,
or the email / text approval) in the job file.

**Estimate / quote**

> This estimate is subject to C&S Plumbing of Lee's Terms & Conditions rev. 2026-11-03,
> available at https://www.csplumbinglee.com/terms and incorporated by reference. Signing,
> paying, scheduling, or authorizing the work constitutes acceptance.

**Bid proposal** — same sentence with "This proposal is subject to …".

**Invoice** — the invoice cites the revision the customer accepted on the estimate or
proposal (clause 1.4), never a newer one. Add the payment lines:

> Credit card payments carry a 3% surcharge, shown as a separate line (credit cards only; no
> surcharge on cash, check, ACH, debit or prepaid). Balances unpaid after the due date accrue
> 1.5% per month. Questions about this invoice: tell us in writing within 10 days of receipt.

Generated by `termsSubjectLine("estimate" | "proposal" | "invoice")` in `src/lib/terms-meta.ts`.

## 2. Citation format

`Terms rev. YYYY-MM-DD` — the value of `TERMS_VERSION`. The revision in effect when the
customer accepts is frozen to that job, including its change orders and invoices. Never
leave it off and never back-fill an old job with a newer revision.

## 3. Publishing a new revision

1. Edit clause text in `src/lib/terms.ts`.
2. Bump `TERMS_VERSION` and `TERMS_EFFECTIVE_DATE` in `src/lib/terms-meta.ts` to the date the
   owners and counsel approved the wording (same date for both).
3. Add a `TERMS_REVISIONS` entry in `src/lib/terms.ts` saying what changed.
4. Deploy. Open `/terms` on the live site, confirm the revision shown is the one approved,
   click **Print / Save as PDF**, and file the PDF in the office Drive as
   `CS-Terms-rev-YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`. That folder is the archive of every published revision;
   the site shows only the current one, and the office sends a prior revision on request.
5. Update the revision string in the invoicing software template, the proposal renderer
   (`cs-proposal-pdf` skill footer and acceptance clause) and `csplumbing-assets`
   `references/payment-terms.md`.
6. Update section 1 of this file.

A Word edit or a branch merge is not publication. The revision is live only when step 4 shows
it on www.csplumbinglee.com.

## 4. Notices that must be printed on the signed document

A link to `/terms` does **not** satisfy these. Section 20 of the Terms describes them for the
customer's information; the statute wants the text in the contract. Counsel confirms the
verbatim text and applicability before the templates are changed.

- **FS 713.015 Construction Lien Law notice.** Direct contract with an owner over $2,500 to
  improve a residence of up to four units. First page of the contract, or a separate page
  signed and dated by the owner. At least 12-point, capitalized, bold. Current text reads
  "YOUR CONTRACTOR MAY ALSO HAVE A LIEN ON YOUR PROPERTY".
- **FS 489.1425 Homeowners' Construction Recovery Fund notice.** Any residential repair,
  restoration, improvement or construction contract over $2,500 (Division II / plumbing is
  covered since July 1, 2016). In the contract, followed by the Construction Industry
  Licensing Board address and phone as set by board rule. Fine up to $500 for a first
  violation, $1,000 after.
- **FS 558.005 Chapter 558 notice.** Contracts for construction or remodeling of a dwelling.
  In the contract, in capital letters, in the form current at signing. Counsel supplies the
  current form; the public page deliberately does not reproduce it.
- **FS 501.031 home solicitation cancellation notice.** Sale solicited and signed at the
  customer's home over $25, unless it falls under an exclusion in FS 501.021 (customer-initiated
  repair or maintenance, or a handwritten dated emergency waiver). Immediately next to the
  buyer's signature, in bold type of at least 10 points, under the heading "BUYER'S RIGHT TO
  CANCEL". The FTC Cooling-Off Rule (16 CFR Part 429) separately requires two copies of a
  "Notice of Cancellation" form; use whichever procedure counsel approves for (a) requested
  repairs, (b) additional work sold during a visit, and (c) emergencies.
- **FS 489.119(5)(b) license number.** Every proposal, contract, invoice and advertisement.
  CFC1432485 and CFC057076.
- **Fixed Price clause (brand rule).** Any document quoting a price for a defined scope.
  Wording is clause 3.3 in `src/lib/terms.ts`.

## 5. Office procedures the Terms rely on

- **Deposits (FS 489.126).** The Terms take no standing deposit. If a proposal states one that
  is more than 10% of the price and a permit is required, apply for the permit within 30 days
  of receiving the deposit and start within 90 days after the permit issues, or get the
  customer's written agreement to a longer period on the proposal.
- **Card surcharge.** Before the first surcharged invoice: notify the processor and card
  networks as they require (Visa requires advance notice), confirm the 3% does not exceed
  our cost of acceptance, show the surcharge as its own line on the invoice and receipt, and
  never surcharge debit or prepaid cards. If the owners choose a cash discount instead, the
  processor still has to approve that arrangement and its disclosures.
- **Notice to Owner.** Serve within 45 days of first furnishing on every job where C&S is not
  in direct contract with the owner; log the service date and the lien deadline per job.
- **Lien waivers.** Conditional waiver (FS 713.20 form) in exchange for each progress or final
  payment; unconditional only after the payment clears.
- **Invoice disputes.** Customers have 10 days from receipt to dispute in writing (clause 7.8);
  the office answers in writing within 10 days. Refunds on cancellation go out within 10
  business days (clauses 14.2, 14.3).
- **Accepted copies.** Keep the accepted estimate or proposal and the approval (signature,
  email or text) with the job file, together with the revision it cites.
- **Marketing photos.** Separate written permission before any job-site photo is used in
  marketing (clause 18.6).
